KIVUSTAY - XPATH QUERY DOCUMENTATION
====================================

XML SOURCE: hotels.xml
PURPOSE: Extracting useful accommodation information
using XPath expressions and predicates.

QUERY 1: Find hotels with a rating of at least 4.0

XPath:
/hotels/hotel[@rating >= 4.0]

Explanation:
Selects all hotels whose rating is greater than or
equal to 4.0.

QUERY 2: Find hotels located in Gisenyi

XPath:
/hotels/hotel[location='Gisenyi']

Explanation:
Selects hotels whose location is exactly Gisenyi.

QUERY 3: Find rooms with a capacity of at least 3 guests

XPath:
/hotels/hotel/rooms/room[@capacity >= 3]

Explanation:
Selects rooms that can accommodate three or more guests.

QUERY 4: Find all confirmed bookings

XPath:
/hotels/hotel/rooms/room/bookings/booking[status='CONFIRMED']

Explanation:
Selects bookings whose status is CONFIRMED.

QUERY 5: Find available rooms with a price below 100

XPath:
/hotels/hotel/rooms/room[@status='AVAILABLE'
and pricePerNight < 100]

Explanation:
Selects rooms that are available and cost less than
100 per night.
